About

How I got here

In college I spent two years volunteering with the Twin City Harm Reduction Coalition in Winston-Salem: packing bags for mobile delivery, assembling naloxone kits, and leading trainings. I also served on the Forsyth Regional Opioid & Substance Use Team. After graduating from Wake Forest with a degree in sociology, I worked for two years with the NC Formerly Incarcerated Transition Program, supporting people with substance use disorders as they left prison and reconnected with care.

That work showed me how often there's a gap between the services that exist on paper and the services people can actually reach. I'm now a PhD student in Health Behavior at UNC Gillings, advised by Dr. Vivian Go, and I study that gap with qualitative and mixed methods. I want to bring the same work to product teams, especially in digital health and behavior change, though I'm interested in any team trying to fit a product into people's real routines.
